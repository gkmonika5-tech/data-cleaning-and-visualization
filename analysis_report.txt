6. Key observations
========================================================================
1. Study hours per week has the strongest positive link with the average score (correlation r = 0.71): students who study more score higher.
2. Age (years) has almost no linear link with the average score (r = -0.01).
3. Students who attend extra classes average 60.6 marks versus 57.6 for those who do not (difference +3.1).
4. Students with internet access average 59.6 marks versus 56.7 without it (difference +3.0).
5. By school type, Private schools have the highest average (61.2) and Government schools the lowest (57.2).
6. Average score rises with parental education, from 57.6 (High School) to 63.2 (Master's).
7. Average score by study level: Low = 48.9, Medium = 56.2, High = 66.9.
8. Science score is the strongest subject (mean 59.8) and English score the weakest (mean 58.0).
9. Average scores of Female (59.0) and Male (58.9) students differ by only 0.1 marks, so gender is not a major factor.
10. 43.4% of students earn grade A or B, while 19.6% fall in grade D or F.
11. Students with attendance of 85% or more average 59.2 marks; those below 85% average 58.6.
